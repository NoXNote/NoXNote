## Petril, Crypto Game Jam

![Atmospheric Setting](./petril1.gif)

*Atmospheric Setting*

I focused on the atmosphere of this game. The gameplay is accessed via the central terminal, so I had to make sure the little you see was as nice as possible. 

![Terminal System](./petril2.gif)

*Terminal System*

You can manipulate the stock market through various chat windows. You get a small kickback per transaction of your coin, Petril. A desktop and windowing environment is fully modelled.

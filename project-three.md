## Don't Hit Me, Card Game Jam

![Platforming, with hazards](./dont.gif)

*Platforming, with hazards*

![Dangerous features of levels](./dont2.gif)

*Dangerous features of levels*

There is more than just platforming involved. You have to dodge blowing fans, bounce pads, spikes, and automatic turrets.
